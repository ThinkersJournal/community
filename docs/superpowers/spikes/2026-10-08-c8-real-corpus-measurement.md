# C8 real-corpus measurement: share of PDQ quality <= 49 on real screenshots, diagrams and flat-art images

**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Part of #114.** Third spike run, after `2026-10-08-imagemagick-pdq-spike.md` (pass 1) and
`2026-10-08-imagemagick-systemd-spike-pass-2.md` (pass 2, whose C8 used only a generated set). Evidence for
`../specs/2026-10-08-vps-image-service-design.md` section 7 item 9. Provider named only as HMS-A. No HMS-A call, no database.

**The images are deliberately NOT committed.** Several are CC BY or CC BY-SA, whose attribution and licence terms apply to
redistribution. This document records title, licence, author and URL per image (the manifest below) so each can be fetched again
from its own page.

## Method

A throwaway container from the pass-1 image (`--rm`, `--network none`, corpus mounted read-only). Each file goes through the
approved scan-frame pipeline: `magick <fmt>:fd:0[0 for gif/webp] -alpha off -colorspace sRGB [-define sample:offset=0 -sample
512x512!] -depth 8 rgb:fd:1`, no auto-orient, **no resize when both sides are <= 512**. Then the reference PDQ core (same build as
pass 2, section 5; `facebook/ThreatExchange` `85978d7`, ImageMagick 7.1.1-43 as in pass 1) reports the quality. Any ImageMagick
stderr or non-zero exit would be recorded as an error: 0 occurred. Corpus: 91 files from Wikimedia Commons (search per category,
file namespace, bitmap formats, size <= 3 MB, licence CC0 / public domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA). Dimensions are of the original file
as downloaded, not a Commons thumbnail.

## Result

**0 of 91 images have quality <= 49 (0.0%). Wilson 95% interval for the overall share: 0.0% to 4.1%.** With a zero count the upper
bound is the informative part, and it bounds THIS selection process, not user uploads.

Quality distribution (n = 91): min 77, p10 100, p25 100, median 100, p75 100, max 100. 88 of 91 are exactly 100. The three lowest:
`pixel_art_00.png` = 77 (1216x1216, sampled down), `terminal_code_07.png` = 78 (800x480, sampled down), `ui_screenshot_07.png` = 79
(843x502, sampled down). The next value, 100, is `chart_graph_00.png` (3780x2126, sampled down).

### Positive control (the measurement can fail)

Same container, same pipeline, same PDQ, generated in this run:

| control | quality | would be rejected at <= 49 |
|---|---|---|
| 800x600 flat white PNG | **0** | yes |
| 800x600 red-blue gradient | **0** | yes |
| flat white with one 50x20 black rectangle | **13** | yes |

So the 0-of-91 count is not a broken pipeline. Compare pass 2, where flat logos and gradients generated by the spike failed (that
set is generated, not a screenshot corpus, not a launch estimate).

### Images at quality <= 49

None. The lowest three are pixel art (77), a desktop terminal screenshot (78) and a Firefox-Sync preferences screenshot (79): all
hard-edged blocks or large flat panes, yet well above 49. Members expected to be risky (a flat-blue logo on black, a 57x52 icon, a
64x64 pixel-art GIF, 8-bit sprites) all scored 100. The images were not inspected visually; the remark about the lowest three is
from category and the nature of PDQ quality (gradient energy of the downsampled luma), not from looking.

## Limits (selection bias)

- **Not an upload sample.** A hand-picked set of Wikimedia Commons files chosen by search terms per category: images Commons
  contributors found worth uploading, mostly clean, cropped, high-resolution and encyclopaedic. Real uploads include phone
  screenshots of chat apps, dark-mode UIs with large flat panes, memes with a solid-colour band and a caption, tiny avatars,
  mostly-blank screenshots and mobile-edited images.
- **Mostly-flat images are underrepresented, and they are the kind that fail.** Large flat regions are what drive PDQ quality
  toward 0. Solid-colour memes, one-colour logos, blank editors and dialogs, and phone chat screenshots are not represented here
  (the real logos in this set were detailed enough to pass). The positive control above and the generated set of pass 2 show that
  flat inputs do fail.
- Screenshots skew to Linux/GNOME and Wikimedia sites. **WebP is unmeasured** (none passed the search filters). Animated GIFs are
  measured on **frame 0 only**.
- 7 to 8 images per category.
- **No visual inspection.** Content safety rests on Commons titles and the search terms, with a title blocklist for people,
  sexual and child terms.
- **Five candidates were excluded by title before measuring** (a photo of an event, a government-archive photo, two scans of
  official records that might hold personal data, one news-site homepage). They were excluded by title and suitability, not by
  quality, so they do not bias the share.
- No screenshots of the spike author's own machine were taken, so the typical blank-editor or calculator case is not represented.

## Conclusion

In a hand-picked set of 91 real free-licence screenshots, diagrams, charts, maps, slides, logos, pixel art, line art and scans, none
scored PDQ quality <= 49 (0%; Wilson 95% upper bound 4.1% for this selection). Ordinary content-rich non-photographic images are
therefore unlikely to be refused as unscannable. **This does not show that flat images pass**: mostly-flat images are exactly what
the positive control and the generated set show failing, they are not represented here, and the set is not an upload sample, so
this is **not the production rate.** The share for flat-heavy images (solid-colour memes, one-colour logos, blank editors, phone
chat screenshots) remains unmeasured.
